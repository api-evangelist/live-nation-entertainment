Key Takeaways Ticketmaster launches in Uruguay, bringing its global ticketing technology and fan-first experience to a new market. As part of its launch, Ticketmaster has agreed to acquire RED UTS, combining local market expertise with Ticketmaster’s global technology, innovation and scale. Ticketmaster is investing in the future of live entertainment in Uruguay through world-class technology designed to make discovering, buying and accessing live events simpler, safer and more transparent.
