This week, the Washington, DC City Council passed ticketing reforms that will protect fans, artists and venues. The following statement is attributable to Kelly Flanigan, President, Concerts, DC/ MD/ VA: “We applaud the D.C. City Council for protecting fans and artists by banning speculative ticketing, reaffirming all in pricing, and capping concert ticket resale prices.
